Our Story

Where Art Meets Celebration

A family-run business born from a bride's vision to make luxury wedding florals both beautiful and accessible.

How It Began

From Bride to Business

LilliBloom was born from a very personal place. While planning her own wedding, Esme discovered what many brides come to realize: fresh flowers are stunning, but the cost can be staggering. As a Head of Art teacher with a background in fine art and professional set design, she knew there had to be a better way.

Working alongside her mum, Esme began creating luxury artificial arrangements for her own celebration. The results exceeded every expectation. Guests couldn't tell the difference. The arrangements remained flawless from ceremony to reception. And perhaps most importantly, they became treasured keepsakes rather than wilted memories.

What started as a solution for one wedding quickly became a passion to help other couples experience the same joy, beauty, and peace of mind. LilliBloom was born.
