Our Story

Florals, kept forever.

LilliBloom began with a bride's own wedding — and the discovery that artificial florals, made with care, can hold the day exactly as it was. No wilting. No worry. A bouquet that becomes a keepsake.

We work by appointment, in small numbers, with one principle: every arrangement is the only one we're making that week.
