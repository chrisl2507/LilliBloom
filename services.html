How it works

From first chat to delivery.

Four steps, each clearly explained.

01

Consultation

We talk through your venue, dress, palette, and the feeling you want. Bring inspiration; we'll ask the practical questions.

02

Proposal

A short written proposal: what we'd make, how it'd sit in the room, and the cost. We adjust until it's right.

03

Make

We build everything in the studio, by hand. You see samples and progress along the way.

04

Deliver and set up

Pieces arrive at your venue ready to go. We set up, take a final look, and step away.
