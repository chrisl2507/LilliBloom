How It Works

Our Design Process

1

Initial Consultation

We begin with a detailed conversation about your vision, style, and wedding details. Share your inspiration, and we'll guide you through possibilities.

2

Design Proposal

Based on our consultation, we create a custom design proposal with mood boards, samples, and detailed pricing for your review.

3

Refinement

We refine the designs together, adjusting colors, styles, and details until everything is perfect. Your satisfaction is our priority.

4

Creation & Delivery

We handcraft your arrangements with meticulous care and deliver them to your venue, ensuring everything is flawless for your big day.
