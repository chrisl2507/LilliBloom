Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

Last Updated: November 2025

Who We Are

LilliBloom is a family-run wedding florals business based in Suffolk, UK. We specialize in luxury artificial wedding flowers and operate on an appointment-only basis serving couples throughout Suffolk, Cambridgeshire, and beyond.

For privacy-related questions, contact us at: info@lillibloom.com

What Information We Collect

When you contact us through our website, we collect:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Wedding date (if provided)
  • Any details you share about your wedding vision

How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to:

  • Respond to your inquiry about our services
  • Schedule consultations and communicate about your wedding florals
  • Create and deliver your bespoke floral arrangements
  • Fulfill our contractual obligations if you book our services

We will never: Sell your information, share it with third parties for marketing purposes, or send you unsolicited marketing emails.

Legal Basis for Processing

Under UK GDPR, we process your personal data based on:

  • Consent: When you submit our contact form, you consent to us using your information to respond to your inquiry
  • Contract: If you book our services, processing is necessary to fulfill our contractual obligations
  • Legitimate Interests: Running our business and responding to potential client inquiries

How Long We Keep Your Information

We retain your information as follows:

  • Inquiries only: Up to 2 years, then deleted
  • Booked clients: 7 years (for tax and accounting purposes)
  • Photos of your wedding: Only with your explicit permission, and you can request removal at any time

Cookies & Website Analytics

Our website uses:

  • Essential cookies: Required for the website to function (contact forms, navigation)
  • Google Analytics: To understand how visitors use our site (anonymised data only)

You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though some site features may not work properly.

Your Rights Under UK GDPR

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Rectification: Ask us to correct any inaccurate information
  • Erasure: Request deletion of your data (where legally permissible)
  • Restrict Processing: Ask us to limit how we use your data
  • Data Portability: Receive your data in a common format
  • Object: Object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Withdraw Consent: Withdraw consent at any time (doesn't affect prior processing)

To exercise any of these rights, email us at info@lillibloom.com. We'll respond within one month.

How We Protect Your Information

We take reasonable precautions to protect your data:

  • Secure website hosting with SSL encryption
  • Password-protected email accounts
  • Limited access to personal information (family business only)
  • Regular deletion of outdated inquiries

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services that may process your data:

  • Netlify: Website hosting and form submissions
  • Google Analytics: Website usage statistics (anonymized)
  • Email provider: For storing and responding to inquiries

These services have their own privacy policies and are GDPR compliant.

Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for adults planning weddings. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16. If you believe we've inadvertently collected such information, please contact us immediately.

International Data Transfers

Your data is primarily stored within the UK/EU. Some third-party services (like Netlify) may transfer data internationally, but only to countries with adequate data protection safeguards.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy occasionally. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. For significant changes, we'll notify existing clients directly.

Questions or Complaints

If you have questions or concerns about how we handle your data, please contact us at:

Email: info@lillibloom.com
Subject Line: "Privacy Inquiry"

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the UK's supervisory authority:

Information Commissioner's Office (ICO)
Website: ico.org.uk
Helpline: 0303 123 1113
