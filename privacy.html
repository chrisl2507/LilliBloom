Privacy

Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

Last updated · May 2026

Who we are

LilliBloom is a family-run wedding florals studio based in Suffolk, UK. We work by appointment with a small number of weddings each season, serving couples throughout Suffolk, Cambridgeshire, Essex, and beyond.

For privacy-related questions, contact info@lillibloom.com.

What we collect

When you contact us through our website, we collect:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Wedding date (if provided)
  • Any details you share about your wedding vision

How we use your information

We use your information solely to:

  • Respond to your enquiry about our services
  • Schedule consultations and communicate about your wedding florals
  • Create and deliver your bespoke arrangements
  • Fulfil our contractual obligations if you book our services

We will never sell your information, share it with third parties for marketing, or send unsolicited marketing emails.

Legal basis for processing

Under UK GDPR, we process your personal data based on:

  • Consent — when you submit our contact form, you consent to us using your information to respond.
  • Contract — if you book our services, processing is necessary to fulfil our contractual obligations.
  • Legitimate interests — running our business and responding to potential client enquiries.

How long we keep your information

  • Enquiries only: up to 2 years, then deleted.
  • Booked clients: 7 years (for tax and accounting).
  • Photos of your wedding: only with your explicit permission, removable on request.

Cookies and analytics

Our site uses:

  • Essential cookies — required for the site to function (forms, navigation).
  • Analytics — to understand how visitors use the site (anonymised).

Your rights under UK GDPR

You have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectification — ask us to correct inaccurate information.
  • Erasure — request deletion of your data (where legally permissible).
  • Restrict processing — ask us to limit how we use your data.
  • Data portability — receive your data in a common format.
  • Object — object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Withdraw consent — at any time (doesn't affect prior processing).

To exercise any of these rights, email info@lillibloom.com. We'll respond within one month.

How we protect your information

  • Secure website hosting with SSL encryption
  • Password-protected email accounts
  • Limited access to personal information (family business only)
  • Regular deletion of outdated enquiries

Third-party services

  • Netlify — website hosting and form submissions.
  • Analytics — anonymised website usage statistics.
  • Email provider — for storing and responding to enquiries.

These services have their own privacy policies and are GDPR-compliant.

Children's privacy

Our services are intended for adults planning weddings. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16. If you believe we've inadvertently collected such information, please contact us.

International data transfers

Your data is primarily stored within the UK/EU. Some third-party services may transfer data internationally, but only to countries with adequate data protection safeguards.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy occasionally. Any changes will be posted here with an updated "Last updated" date.

Questions or complaints

If you have questions or concerns about how we handle your data:

Email: info@lillibloom.com
Subject line: "Privacy Inquiry"

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the UK's supervisory authority:

Information Commissioner's Office (ICO)
Website: ico.org.uk
Helpline: 0303 123 1113
